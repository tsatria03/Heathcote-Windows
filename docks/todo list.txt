Add a way to save your progress when you quit, so Continue story carries on from where you left off.

##Finished.

The game opens with the original's police radio and first-run advice, then the main menu with Start story or Continue story, Settings, Credits and Quit, with the title music under it.
The pause menu has Resume, Restart story, Settings and Return to menu.
The story ends with the original's credits, read one at a time.
The original's sounds play: the story's voices, its music and ambiences, and its own interface sounds.
The story can be played from start to end, with its actions menu, rewind, fast-forward and pause, and the recorded choices.
Every volume can be turned up and down with its keys, and the game remembers it.
Every key can be rebound with F1.
The menus are spoken, and move with the arrows, Home and End.
The game speaks through your screen reader.
