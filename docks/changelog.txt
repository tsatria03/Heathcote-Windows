Shift with Home and Shift with End turn the master volume up and down from anywhere in the game.
Page Up and Page Down turn the title volume up and down wherever the title music plays.

F1 opens the key bindings from anywhere, where every action can be rebound, given another key, unbound or reset.

The game opens on a main menu with Settings and Quit.
If an error stops the game, it says so aloud and writes what happened to crash.txt in its logs folder.
The game speaks through NVDA, another screen reader, or a Windows voice.
