The dev builds audio-only games for blind players and reviews everything through NVDA.

**How to apply:**
- In replies and docs, use headings and bulleted lists. Do not use wide tables, which are hard to follow by screen reader. Keep lines short, well under 1024 characters, since longer lines get split mid-thought.
- Ask questions the way [[feedback_one_question_at_a_time]] and [[feedback_no_question_pickers]] say.
- Never run anything that plays sound or speaks through NVDA without the dev's say-so ([[feedback_dont_run_or_build]]).
- Judge game changes by how they sound to a player who cannot see the window. The audio and the speech are the real interface.
