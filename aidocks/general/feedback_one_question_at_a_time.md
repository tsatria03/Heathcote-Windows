When decisions are needed from the dev, ask **one question per reply**, then wait for the answer before asking the next. Never end a reply with a numbered list of several choices to make.

**Why:** The dev's standing rule, adopted for this project on 2026-10-19. They read by screen reader ([[user_screen_reader]]), where a stack of questions is hard to answer.

**How to apply:** Ask as plain text, not through the picker tool ([[feedback_no_question_pickers]]). Pick the question whose answer matters most or blocks the rest, give a recommendation with it, and keep the other questions for later replies.
